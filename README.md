### Who I Am

Data Scientist based in **Nairobi, Kenya**, transitioning into Data Engineering. I'm interested in designing the pipelines, infrastructure, and systems that make data actually useful at scale — particularly across African markets where reliable data infrastructure is still being built.

My background is in machine learning and analytics, which means I think about data systems not just as plumbing, but in terms of what they need to deliver downstream.
